Kisan Dhara - Account Deletion

Use this page to request deletion of your Kisan Dhara account and the personal data associated with it. You do not need to reinstall or sign in to the app to submit a request.

Before you submit: Enter the email address or mobile number registered with your Kisan Dhara account. We may contact you to verify that you own the account. Do not enter your password or OTP.

Submit a deletion request

When you submit, your device's email app will open with the request addressed to infokisandhara@gmail.com. Review and send that email to complete submission. If no email app opens, send your request manually to this address using the registered email or mobile number.

What happens next?

We may verify account ownership before processing the request. Eligible requests will be handled within the period described in the Privacy Policy. Some information may be retained where required by applicable law; any such retention will be limited to that legal purpose.

For details about data collection and handling, read the Kisan Dhara Privacy Policy.
