1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, use, and protection of personal data on the Kisan Dhara mobile application ("App") in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDPA"), DPDP Rules, 2025, and Google Play Store Developer Policies.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to operate the App, including basic account details (Name, Mobile Number, Email, District/State), crop preferences, community posts ("Kisan Madad"), and basic device diagnostics to provide live mandi rates and agricultural awareness.

3. App Permissions

We request Location (optional, for nearby mandi rates), Camera / Photo Gallery (optional, for uploading crop photos in Kisan Madad), and Notifications / SMS (for OTP verification and mandi rate alerts). You can manage or revoke these permissions anytime in your device settings.

4. Data Security & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade user personal data to third parties. Information is stored securely and used solely to deliver App functionality or comply with applicable Indian laws.
